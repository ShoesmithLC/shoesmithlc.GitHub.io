## How We Build

Our team runs on a governed, AI-assisted operating model: proposals move through a defined approval chain before anything ships publicly. Dedicated owners cover finance, legal, product, engineering, marketing, operations, HR, and IT. The result is a small company that moves like an organized, automated one — every piece of public content and every product decision has a clear, accountable owner.
