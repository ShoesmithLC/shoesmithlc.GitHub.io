## How we build

Our team runs on a governed, AI-assisted operating model: proposals move through a defined approval process before anything ships publicly, with dedicated owners for finance, legal, product, engineering, marketing, operations, HR, and IT. The result is a small company that moves like an automated, well-organized one — every piece of public content and every product decision has a clear, accountable owner behind it.
