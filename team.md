# Leadership Team

Shoesmith Life Cycle LLC is led by Robert Shoesmith, Founder & CEO, supported by a leadership team that each owns a functional area of the company.

## Robert Shoesmith — Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Robert is the Founder and CEO of Shoesmith Life Cycle LLC, an e-commerce and automation-driven company selling through Etsy and our own storefront. He sets company-wide strategy and direction, and personally builds much of the operational backbone of the business through workflow automation.

**Focus:** Company vision and strategy · product and store direction · cross-platform automation · final approval on cross-functional initiatives.

## Arthur Shoesmith — Chief Operating Officer (COO)

Arthur runs day-to-day operations, making sure roadmap initiatives set by the CEO execute on schedule. He's the operational glue between departments, translating strategy into a working weekly cadence and flagging execution risk before it becomes a missed deadline.

**Focus:** Operational execution · process and workflow oversight · cross-team coordination.

## Daisy Shoesmith — Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

Daisy owns the books, budgeting, and financial planning for the company. She reviews the financial viability of every strategic initiative before it moves forward, keeping spend aligned with revenue from our e-commerce channels.

**Focus:** Financial planning and reporting · budgeting · vendor and payment management.

## Duke Shoesmith — Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)

Duke owns brand voice, positioning, and marketing strategy across every channel Shoesmith Life Cycle LLC publishes to. He keeps messaging consistent across our social presence, our Etsy storefront, and our own site.

**Focus:** Brand narrative and marketing strategy · content and social distribution · campaign planning.

## Cooper Shoesmith — Chief Information Officer (CIO)

Cooper is responsible for the internal data systems, archives, and information security practices that keep company operations running safely, including access and permissions hygiene across our tools.

**Focus:** Data systems and records management · internal tooling · information security.

## Dallas Shoesmith — Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

Dallas owns the technical platform and engineering direction for the company, including the automation pipelines that keep our internal tooling running smoothly.

**Focus:** Engineering and platform architecture · automation and integration · technical strategy.

## NALA Shoesmith — Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO)

NALA owns the people function at Shoesmith Life Cycle LLC — onboarding, offboarding, and keeping the team roster and records accurate and up to date.

**Focus:** People operations · onboarding and offboarding · team records.

## Ozzy Shoesmith — Chief Product Officer (CPO)

Ozzy owns the product roadmap and prioritization for the company, coordinating closely with Engineering and Operations so that roadmap-driven changes land smoothly.

**Focus:** Product roadmap and prioritization · cross-functional coordination · project scoping.

## Wilder Shoesmith — Chief Legal Officer (CLO)

Wilder owns contracts, compliance, and legal review for the company, serving as the compliance checkpoint that keeps our data-handling and business practices defensible as we grow.

**Focus:** Contract and compliance review · data privacy · legal risk assessment.
